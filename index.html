The Problem

Managing a school should not be this hard

Most Nigerian school owners are still tracking fees in notebooks, chasing parents on the phone, and writing results by hand. There is a better way.

  • 📒 Fee records scattered across notebooks — hard to track who has paid and who is owing
  • 📞 Calling parents one by one to remind them about unpaid fees wastes hours every week
